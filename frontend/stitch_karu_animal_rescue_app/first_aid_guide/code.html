Emergency Steps: Fracture

1

Secure the Scene

Ensure the animal is in a safe, quiet location away from traffic. Use a leash or towel to gently restrain them.

2

Minimize Movement

Do not try to "set" the bone. Keep the animal as still as possible on a firm, flat surface like a board or thick cardboard.

3

Control Bleeding

If the bone has broken the skin, apply very light pressure with a clean cloth to stop bleeding without moving the limb.

block

Do NOT do

  • Do NOT try to splint the limb unless you are trained.
  • Do NOT give human painkillers (like Paracetamol/Ibuprofen) as they are toxic.
  • Do NOT force the animal to walk or move the injured part.
emergency_home

When to call a vet

Immediate professional help is required if the limb is hanging at an odd angle, if the animal is in extreme vocal pain, or if there is visible bone exposure.
